Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 6, 2026

Run Visuals is operated by Finish Line Labs, LLC ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Run Visuals mobile application (the "App").

Information We Handle

Activity Data

When you connect a third-party fitness service or enter data manually, the following activity information passes through our servers or is stored on your device:

  • Distance, duration, pace, speed, and split times
  • GPS route data (polylines)
  • Elevation and heart rate data
  • Activity date and type (including running, walking, hiking, and cycling)

Third-Party Service Accounts

If you choose to connect your Garmin or Strava account:

  • Garmin Connect: Garmin sends activity data to our servers via its webhook system. This data is temporarily stored on our servers until the App fetches it to your device.
  • Strava: Activity data is fetched from the Strava API. Activity summaries are temporarily stored on our servers until fetched by the App; detailed activity data is cached locally on your device.

We only access running, walking, hiking, and cycling activities. We do not access other workout types, social data, or profile information beyond what is needed to identify your activities.

If you choose to connect Apple Health (iOS only), activity data is read directly on your device. No Apple Health data is sent to or stored on our servers.

Photos and Camera

If you choose to add a background image to your card, the App may request access to your device's camera or photo library. Photos you select are used locally to generate your card image. We do not upload or store your photos on our servers.

Analytics Data

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data, including:

  • Screen views
  • Feature usage (which card/sticker designs are used, data sources selected)
  • Card downloads and shares

This data is aggregated and anonymous. It does not include your activity data, personal details, or photos. Firebase Analytics may collect device identifiers and IP addresses as described in Google's Firebase Privacy documentation.

How We Use Your Information

  • To generate visual cards and stickers from your activity data
  • To temporarily store your activities on our servers until they are fetched by the App on your device
  • To improve the App based on anonymous, aggregated usage patterns

Data Storage and Security

  • On your device: Activity data is cached locally using on-device storage.
  • On our servers: Activity data from Garmin and Strava is temporarily stored on our servers (Amazon DynamoDB, hosted in AWS US East) until it is fetched by the App on your device. Once the data is successfully fetched to your device, it is immediately deleted from our servers. Any activity data that is not fetched by the App will be automatically deleted from our servers after 30 days. OAuth tokens for connected services are stored securely on our servers.

We use HTTPS for all communication between the App and our servers.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Service providers: We use AWS for server hosting and database storage, Google Firebase for analytics, and Google Maps for map imagery.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights.

Artificial Intelligence

AI-powered tools (such as Claude by Anthropic) were used to assist with the development and coding of the App. However, the App itself does not use, incorporate, or rely on any artificial intelligence, machine learning models, or AI-powered systems in its functionality. Your data is never processed by or sent to any AI system.

Your Choices

  • Disconnect accounts: You can disconnect your Garmin or Strava account at any time through the App or the respective service's settings.
  • Camera and photo access: You can deny or revoke camera/photo permissions in your device settings. The App will still function without these permissions.
  • Delete your data: To request deletion of your data from our servers, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy within the App or on our website. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
