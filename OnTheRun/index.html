On the Run!

A match-3 race through six cities. For iPhone and Android.

Every gear you clear moves your runner down the road. Match running gear, build specials, pack your Fuel, and reach the finish line before your moves run out.

In the game

  • Match gear, cover the miles: every clear adds distance; cascades, specials and broken rocks add more.
  • Six cities: Bay City, Neon Circuit, Wine Country, Crownbridge and Angel City, with Track Town for training: nearly 100 levels.
  • Fuel: pack boosts before the start and use more mid-race.
  • Your real steps count: connect Apple Health or Health Connect and your walks and runs earn Race Coins and Stamina. Optional, and your health data stays on your phone.
  • Free to play, with no ads. Optional in-app purchases.
