Privacy Policy for Media Stitch

Effective Date: January 2, 2026

Overview

Media Stitch is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Media Stitch operates entirely on your device and does not transmit, store, or collect any personal data.

Data Storage

  • Local Storage Only: All projects, photos, videos, and settings are stored locally on your device
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not upload or store your content on any external servers
  • No Account Required: The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal information

Photos and Videos

  • Device Access: The app requests permission to access your photo library to create video projects
  • Local Processing: All photo and video processing happens locally on your device
  • Your Control: You maintain full control over your media - we cannot access it remotely

In-App Purchases

  • Apple Handles Transactions: All premium purchases are processed by Apple's App Store
  • No Financial Data: We do not collect or store any payment information
  • Purchase History: Apple manages your purchase history, not us

Analytics and Tracking

  • No Analytics: We do not use any analytics services
  • No Tracking: We do not track your behavior or usage patterns
  • No Advertising: The app contains no advertisements or tracking pixels

Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Photo Library Access: To select photos and videos for your projects
  • Camera Access: To capture new photos and videos (if applicable)

These permissions are only used for core app functionality and data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

We do not integrate with any third-party services except:

  • Apple App Store: For app distribution and in-app purchases
  • Apple StoreKit: For premium feature purchases

Data Security

Since all data remains on your device:

  • Your Responsibility: Data security depends on your device's security settings
  • No Transmission: No data is transmitted over the internet
  • Local Backup: Your projects are included in your device's backup (iTunes/iCloud) if enabled

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since we collect no personal information from any users, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted in the app and on this page with a new effective date.

Your Rights

Since we collect no personal data:

  • There is no personal data to delete, modify, or export
  • All your content remains under your complete control on your device
